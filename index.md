# CoverKeep Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 12 November 2026

CoverKeep is a warranty tracker. It is designed so your items and receipts stay on your iPhone.

## What we collect

We do not collect your items, receipts, photos or any content you add, and CoverKeep has no accounts, analytics or advertising SDKs.

The only information that leaves your device relates to CoverKeep Premium. To sell and verify subscriptions the app uses RevenueCat (revenuecat.com), which receives an anonymous app user ID generated on your device, your purchase and subscription status, and basic device and app information needed to process purchases. Payments themselves are handled by Apple. We use this only to turn Premium on and off and to restore your purchase. RevenueCat's own privacy policy applies to the data it processes.

## What the app stores on your device

- The items you add (name, brand, dates, price, store, serial number, notes, support contacts).
- Receipts and photos you attach.
- Your settings.

This data lives in the app's storage on your device. If you use iCloud Backup, iOS may include it in your device backup; that is controlled by Apple and your iCloud settings.

## Permissions

- **Camera and photos:** only to attach receipts and product photos you choose.
- **Notifications:** only to remind you before a warranty ends. Reminders are scheduled on your device.
- **Face ID:** only if you turn on app lock; the app never receives your biometric data.

## Sharing

The app shares data only when you ask it to, for example when you export a spreadsheet or share a receipt with another app.

## Deleting your data

Delete an item to remove it and its receipts from the device, or delete the app to remove everything. Purchase records held by Apple and RevenueCat are governed by their policies; you can manage a subscription in your Apple ID settings.

## Children

CoverKeep is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
